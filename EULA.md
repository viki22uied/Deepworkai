## 2. What the Software does on your PC

To work, the Software runs a background app on your Windows PC that:

- notes which app window is in front and its (redacted) title, to estimate your focus;
- saves snapshots of your open windows, their positions and your open browser tabs;
- reopens apps, browser tabs and windows when you ask it to restore a saved moment;
- moves and resizes your windows during a restore;
- runs the terminal commands you place in Quick Commands, in your current project folder;
- if you add your own tokens, talks to Slack, GitHub or Jira on your behalf.

Restoring can open, reopen, move or resize windows and tabs, and Quick Commands run exactly what
you configure. **Save your work before using restore or running commands.** The Software cannot
close browser tabs, and it cannot recover anything you have not saved.
